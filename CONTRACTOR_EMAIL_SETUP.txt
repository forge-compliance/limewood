Limewood Engineering v7.2 - Contractor Email Setup

Uses the same Cloudflare/Resend variables as Maintenance Issues:
SUPABASE_URL
SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY
RESEND_API_KEY
MAINTENANCE_FROM_EMAIL

Add:
MAINTENANCE_NOTIFY_EMAIL
  Internal email address that should receive contractor quote-arrival notifications.

Workflow:
1. Add contractors in Contractors & Quotes.
2. Open a maintenance issue and choose Request contractor quotes.
3. Select approved contractors.
4. Each contractor receives a secure link.
5. Contractor submits Repair or Replacement proposal, cost, lead time and warranty.
6. Quotes appear in Contractors & Quotes for comparison.
7. Approve a quote.
8. Contractor receives approval/instruction email.
9. Approved amount becomes committed spend for future budget reporting.
